Software mitigations play a critical role in the quest to secure the digital world. Shortly after the discovery and the rise of buffer overflows in the 90s, mitigations were introduced in the software ecosystem and eventually made their way into virtually any piece of software we run on our devices: from browsers to web servers, from OS kernels to userspace applications. Mitigations are typically designed to address one or more classes of vulnerabilities, making their exploitation more difficult.
